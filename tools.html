Live diagnostics

Four pages that answer four different questions about the job pipeline. Every one of them fetches from the real job-board APIs when you open it — none of them read a cache or a saved snapshot, so what you see is the state right now.

Seattle / LA coverage audit coverage-seattle-la.html

Answers: which companies are actually hiring in my two hubs, and how many roles each has. Also lists every location string the app could not place — that list is where coverage gaps show up first.

 ATS token probe token-probe.html

Answers: are the job boards I already have configured still alive? Walks every token in companies.json and reports the ones that stopped responding. Run this when a company you expect goes quiet.

 Cross-ATS route finder heal-probe.html

Answers: can I find a job board for the companies that don't have one? Takes the needs_route list and hunts each company across 23 public ATS APIs, trying hyphenated, dotted, suffixed and PascalCase token forms plus post-acquisition names.

 Match-score sanity check score-proof.html

Answers: is the résumé match actually reading job descriptions, or just titles? Scores live postings both ways side by side. If the two columns ever converge, description extraction has broken again.
These pages have to be opened over http://, not by double-clicking the file — the coverage audit reads index.html and the probes read companies.json, and a browser blocks both over file://. On the live site they just work.
